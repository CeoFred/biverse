Biverse - live Bible verse projection from speech
==================================================

Free, offline. Listens to the preacher, recognises Bible references
("John chapter three verse sixteen") and shows the verse full-screen on
the projector.

Start:   Windows - open the Biverse folder and run Biverse.exe
         macOS   - right-click Biverse.app > Open the first time
                   (it is not signed by Apple)

Keep the whole Biverse folder together: Biverse.exe needs the files next
to it (the Bible database and the speech model).

Windows 7: needs Service Pack 1 and update KB2999226 (Universal C Runtime).
Use the "windows-legacy" download on Windows 7/8.

Optional models (Parakeet, AI sermon summary): Settings > Models > Download,
or the pack zips from the Releases page (Install from a file...).
The AI summary works on Windows 10/11 64-bit,
macOS and Linux x64 only; not on Windows 7/8 or 32-bit Windows.

Full guide: USER_GUIDE.md in the project, or
https://github.com/CeoFred/biverse/blob/main/USER_GUIDE.md
(Biverse only connects to the internet when you press Download for a model
in Settings > Models.)
